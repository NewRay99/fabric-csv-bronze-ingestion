# Dashboard Design Notes

## Executive page

Top row: New referrals, Open referrals, Placed by target, Critical overdue, IPAs issued, and Estimated committed cost.

Middle row: twelve-month demand trend, month-end open workload trend, and open referrals by target status.

Bottom row: target hit rate by urgency, referral-to-IPA funnel, board-attention table, and provider response performance.

## Visual style

Use warm neutral panels, dark charcoal type and linework, restrained coral accents for demand and risk, and generous whitespace. Keep charts direct and annotate the target definition. A KPI should always include its denominator or qualifying text where a percentage could be misunderstood.

## Interaction

Provide reporting-period and urgency filters. Keep current-period versus prior-period comparisons clearly labelled, especially for partial months. Board attention tables should show referral ID, urgency, required date, days overdue, and the primary barrier.
