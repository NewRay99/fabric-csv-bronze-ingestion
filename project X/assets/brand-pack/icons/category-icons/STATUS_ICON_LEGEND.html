Status icons

The filled tiles encode the recorded status, not a calculated journey position. Read them alongside the unchanged status label. Closed does not mean successful.

Draft — one amber tile
Open / active — one teal tile
Assigned — two blue tiles across the top
Pending / awaiting — upper-right amber tile
Offer made / submitted — two orange tiles down the left
Under offer — three orange tiles
Accepted / completed / signed — green tiles with a final check
Closed — four solid dark tiles; does not imply success
Cancelled — three red tiles and a solid final dot
Withdrawn — three amber tiles and a final minus
Declined / rejected — three red tiles and a final cross
Excluded — three grey tiles and a final cross
Inactive — three grey tiles and a solid final dot
Missing / unrecognised — grey question mark; original text retained

Missing or unrecognised values use a question mark. All icons are embedded in the report theme, with no external image lookup.
