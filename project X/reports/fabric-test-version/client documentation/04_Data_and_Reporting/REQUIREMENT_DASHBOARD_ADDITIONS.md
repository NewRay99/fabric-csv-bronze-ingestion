## Expiry semantics and provenance

`expired_docs` is a **measure at document grain**, not a new physical Gold column. It returns 1 when a recorded expiry date is before `TODAY()`, 0 when a recorded date is today or later, and blank when the date is absent or the context contains multiple documents. `Document expiry status` distinguishes Unknown expiry, Expired, Expiring in 30 days and Not expired. TODAY follows the Power BI evaluation environment; source timestamps and Service/day-boundary behaviour need runtime acceptance.

The existing expiry counts are reused: expiry < today for expired; today through today + 30 inclusive for soon to expire. Blank dates are excluded by those existing counts. A count can remain blank under the existing DISTINCTCOUNT semantics; the selector displays — rather than manufacturing zero. Empty/no-match contexts do not imply completeness.

Python/Spark source: `05_gold_dimensions.py:239–248` copies current `silver.provider_submission_docs` into `gold.dim_provider_submission_document` through `latest_dimension`, keyed by document_id. No new ETL or historical reconstruction was added. The complete current DAX and retained Python/Spark producer spans are in [KPI_LATEST_CALCULATIONS.md](KPI_LATEST_CALCULATIONS.md).
