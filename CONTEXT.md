# WMPP placement reporting

West Midlands placement reporting follows referrals, provider engagement, offers and individual placement agreements.

## Language

**Referral**:
A request to find a suitable placement, with its own lifecycle and identifier. One referral can have multiple provider assignments and offers.

**Provider assignment**:
The link between a referral and a provider approached about that referral. Assignment does not itself mean that the provider made an offer.

**Offer**:
A proposed placement linked to a referral and a provider, potentially identifying a provider home. Its status is distinct from the referral's current status.

**Referral snapshot**:
A retained view of referral state for a reporting month. A count of closed referrals in a snapshot is distinct from a count of closure events during that month.

**Estimated active weekly cost**:
The estimated weekly liability for active placements. It is not cumulative expenditure or actual total spend.
